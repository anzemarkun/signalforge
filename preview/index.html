02What we do

Commercial clarity, not more activity.

Most industrial sales teams are not short on activity — they're short on signal. Wrong accounts, vague ICPs, and over-broad outreach quietly burn quarters.

Signal Forge sits between strategy and execution. The work is focused, analytical, and built around your specific market — so your sales team spends time on accounts that can actually close.

03Problems we solve

If any of this sounds familiar.

Vague ICP, broad outreach

Your team is calling everyone who looks vaguely relevant. Conversion is patchy and the pipeline is hard to forecast.

Wrong accounts in pipeline

Deals stall because the use case never really fit. Sales hours are spent qualifying out, not qualifying in.

New market, no map

You're considering Germany, the Nordics, or Central Europe — but you don't yet know who the real buyers are, or who already serves them.

Inbound is noisy

Leads arrive but you can't tell which ones deserve a senior conversation. Reps default to first-come-first-served.

Founder-led sales doesn't scale

The founder closes everything — and that's becoming the ceiling. You need a target list that anyone capable can work.

Sector shift, unclear positioning

Your market is moving (defense, energy, reshoring). You need a current read on demand and competitors, not last year's deck.
